Kestrel Station — Life Aboard

1. The Station
TL;DR: Kestrel Station is a mining platform in orbit around a gas giant. Its crew rotates every ninety days and lives by the Ration Ledger.

Kestrel Station hangs above the storms of a gas giant and skims helium from the upper clouds. Around four hundred people live aboard on ninety-day rotations. Air, water and food are tracked in the Ration Ledger, and every crew member checks their allowance at the start of each shift.

---

2. Vacuum Drills
TL;DR: Every crew member practises a vacuum drill each week. A failed drill grounds them from outer-ring work until they pass again.

# The Drill
When the klaxon sounds twice, crew seal their section and pull on emergency hoods within thirty seconds. The drill officer times every person.

# Failing a Drill
A crew member who misses the time limit is grounded from outer-ring work until they pass a retest.

---

3. The Spinward Market
TL;DR: The Spinward Market is the station's only open market, where off-shift crew trade spare ration credits.

Off-shift crew meet in the Spinward Market to trade spare ration credits, music and homegrown herbs. Station security tolerates the market as long as nobody trades oxygen.
