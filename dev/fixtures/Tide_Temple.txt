The Tide Temple — The Sea and the Abyss
Doc ID: TT-001


1. The Sea
TL;DR: The Sea is the living ocean the Tide Temple serves. Priests speak of the Sea as a goddess, never as water.

The Tide Temple teaches that the Sea is a goddess who remembers every ship lost to her. Sailors who swear by the Sea keep their oaths, because the priests say she hears them. A priest pours a cup of salt water on the altar each dawn as an offering to the Sea.

---

2. The Abyss
TL;DR: The Abyss is the dark realm beneath the Sea where drowned oaths go. Only Tide priests may speak its true name.

Below the Sea lies the Abyss, where every broken oath sinks. The priests say the Abyss is patient and hungry, and that a sailor who breaks an oath sworn by the Sea will be pulled down into the Abyss when he dies.
