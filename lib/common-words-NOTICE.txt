common-words.txt is the "2of12inf" list from 12dicts 6.0.2 by Alan Beale
(http://wordlist.aspell.net/12dicts/), lowercased with its marker symbols removed.
Lore Graph uses it only to tell ordinary English words ("tree", "calling") from invented
lore names ("Venari", "manacite").

Alan Beale releases the 12dicts lists to the public domain and asks for acknowledgment.
